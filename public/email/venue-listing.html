Put more of your events in front of Deaf audiences

Hi there,

Deaf people can't book what they can't see. Many don't know an interpreter is possible, so they don't buy a ticket, and you never see the demand.

The PI Events app is where Deaf audiences look for BSL and ISL events. Until now it has mostly shown the shows we've been booked for. We'd like to list more of yours, including events that don't have an interpreter yet. It's free, and every listing is shared on our Instagram and Facebook.
✓ A new audience, at no cost. Your event appears alongside hundreds of interpreted shows Deaf people are already browsing.
✓ Extra reach on social. New listings go out on our Instagram and Facebook with your event artwork.
✓ Clear for everyone. Events without an interpreter yet are marked “Request Interpreter”, so no one is promised something that isn't booked.
✓ Visible inclusion. It shows Deaf fans they're welcome at your events.

How it works

1. Deaf fans find you

They browse by category, city and date.

2. They ask early

A ready-written request goes to your access team, with us copied in.

3. We help you deliver

We can arrange the interpreters, and the listing then shows who's booked.

Only on PI Events

The Communication Tool Kit

Deaf fans get full-screen cards to show your staff on the day: to order at the bar, ask for help, turn speech into text, or explain what they need. They can also create their own.

Good for your team too: quicker, clearer conversations at the bar, the gate and front of house, with no need to know sign language.

All we need from you

• Your events page link, or a list of upcoming events
• Your access team's email, for requests
• Whether you hold accessible seats for BSL users

We only list events that are already publicly announced, ideally at least six weeks away so there's time to arrange an interpreter.
